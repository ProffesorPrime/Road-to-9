# Road to 9

IGCSE and GCSE maths practice with Professor Prime, an AI tutor that gives hints instead of answers.

- **Students** (invite only) practise 45 topic "stops" grouped into grades 1 to 9 (following the Maths Genie IGCSE grade lists), earn XP, streaks and badges, and get help from Professor Prime.
- **Professor Prime leads a short lesson** for every stop, Synthesis-style: he explains, the student plays with an interactive visual (number line, balance scale, fraction bar, percentage grid, ratio bars, Pythagoras squares, triangle labelling, factor pairs), answers quick checks, watches a worked example, does one together and one solo.
- **Grade Check**: a 10-question adaptive test that finds where each student's road starts.
- **Arcade**: 60-second quick-fire mental maths with high scores.
- **Read aloud**: Prime can speak every line, using the browser's built-in voice.
- **Parents** log in to see their child's progress report and a written summary.
- **The tutor** invites students and parents, sees everyone's progress, and can read Prime conversations for safeguarding.

Answers are checked on the server, and the correct answers never reach the browser.

**To launch it, follow [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).**

Built with Next.js, Supabase and the Claude API.
