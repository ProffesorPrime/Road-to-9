# Road to 9

IGCSE and GCSE maths practice with Professor Prime, an AI tutor that gives hints instead of answers.

- **Students** (invite only) practise 11 topic "stops" from grade 4 to 8, earn XP, streaks and badges, and get help from Professor Prime.
- **Parents** log in to see their child's progress report and a written summary.
- **The tutor** invites students and parents, sees everyone's progress, and can read Prime conversations for safeguarding.

Answers are checked on the server, and the correct answers never reach the browser.

**To launch it, follow [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).**

Built with Next.js, Supabase and the Claude API.
